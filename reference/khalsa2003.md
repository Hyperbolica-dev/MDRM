# Khalsa et al. (2003)

SB Khalsa, ME Jewett, C Cajochen, CA Czeisler. A phase response curve to single bright light pulses in human subjects. Journal of Physiology. 2003;549(Pt 3):945-952.

Key finding: Constructed a human phase response curve (PRC) to bright light (~9,500 lux) showing delay zone in evening/early night and advance zone in late night/morning, with magnitude up to 2 hours.

Relevance to MRDM: Provides empirical basis for timing light interventions to shift phase (P). Morning light advances phase, evening light delays phase.
