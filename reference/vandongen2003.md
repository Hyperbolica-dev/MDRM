# Van Dongen et al. (2003)

HPA van Dongen, G Maislin, JM Mullington, DF Dinges. Sleep 26(2):117-126.

Key finding: Chronic sleep restriction to 4h or 6h per night for 14 consecutive days produced cognitive deficits equivalent to 1-2 nights of total sleep deprivation. Subjects showed no awareness of accumulating deficits.

Relevance to MRDM: Chronic sleep debt does not fully resolve after a single recovery night. Supports proportional recovery over absolute recovery (R_max).
