# Banks & Dinges (2010)

S Banks, DF Dinges. Behavioral Sleep Medicine.

Key finding: Recovery from chronic sleep restriction is slower than commonly assumed. One or two nights of extended recovery sleep (8-10h) is insufficient to fully reverse cumulative cognitive deficits accumulated over multiple days of restriction.

Relevance to MRDM: Absolute recovery models (e.g., R_max=3h) are overly optimistic. Multi-night proportional recovery better matches observed recovery trajectories.
