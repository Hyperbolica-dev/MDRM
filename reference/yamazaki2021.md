# Yamazaki et al. (2021)

EM Yamazaki, DL Muto, A Antle, et al. Scientific Reports.

Key finding: Single extended recovery sleep does not eliminate cumulative cognitive performance deficits accumulated during chronic sleep restriction. Recovery requires multiple consecutive nights of adequate sleep.

Relevance to MRDM: Supports proportional debt recovery where D decays fractionally per night, not by a fixed absolute amount.
